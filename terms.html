1. What the app does

OBD App reads diagnostic information from your vehicle through an OBD-II adapter and explains it. The explanations, severity ratings, likely causes, repair difficulty, reset guides and estimates (such as horsepower or fuel economy) are general information, not a diagnosis. Always have a qualified mechanic confirm a problem before repairing it.

2. Safe use

  • Don't look at or touch your phone while driving. Set it up before you start, in a mount, or have a passenger use it.
  • Performance timing is only for closed courses where it's legal and safe. Obey all traffic laws.
  • If a warning light flashes or the car behaves unusually, stop when it's safe.
  • Use an adapter in good condition and unplug it if your car's battery is low or the car will sit unused.

3. Clearing codes

Clearing codes turns off warning lights but doesn't fix the cause, and it resets emissions readiness. Only clear codes after a repair. Never clear airbag (SRS) or brake (ABS) codes to hide a fault. You're responsible for the condition and roadworthiness of your vehicle.
