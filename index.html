Safe by design

Reads, doesn't modify

OBD App reads your car's diagnostic data. It never codes modules, changes mileage or touches emissions equipment. Clearing codes always asks you first.

Private

Scans stay on your iPhone. Optional anonymous car data never includes your VIN, name or location. Read the privacy policy.

Honest

For information only, not a substitute for a mechanic. Don't use the app while driving.
