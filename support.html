Which adapter do I need?

A Bluetooth LE (4.0 or newer) OBD-II adapter. iPhone can't use the older "Bluetooth 3.0" adapters sold for Android, and Wi-Fi adapters take over your phone's internet.

Getting connected

  1. Find the 16-pin port under the dashboard on the driver's side, near the steering column.
  2. Plug the adapter in firmly. Turn the ignition on (the engine can stay off).
  3. Open OBD App and tap Connect Adapter. Don't pair it in iPhone's Bluetooth settings: the app connects by itself.

Common questions

The app can't find my adapter
  • Make sure it's a Bluetooth LE adapter and its light is on.
  • Check Bluetooth is on and allowed for OBD App (Settings ▸ OBD App ▸ Bluetooth).
  • If you paired it in iPhone's Bluetooth settings, tap Forget This Device there, then try again in the app.
  • Unplug the adapter for 10 seconds and plug it back in.
The adapter connects but the car doesn't answer

Turn the ignition fully on (dashboard lights on). Some cars need the engine running. Cars sold in the US from 1996, EU petrol cars from 2001 and EU diesels from 2004 support OBD-II. Send us the Adapter Log if it still fails.

My check-engine light is on but the app shows no codes

Run a new scan with the ignition on. Some cars report the light from a different module: Full Scan (Pro) reads every module. Permanent codes are listed separately in the Codes tab.

Will it work with my car?

Basic diagnostics work with any OBD-II car. Car Sensors adds extra readings for about 880 models worldwide. Full Scan works on CAN cars (most cars from 2008). Heavy trucks with the 9-pin J1939 connector aren't supported yet.

Can the app reset my oil or service light?

The Reset Guides in the Car tab show the dashboard steps for your brand. Most cars reset the reminder from the instrument cluster, with no tool needed.

Is it safe to clear codes?

Yes, after the problem is fixed. Clearing doesn't repair anything: if the fault is still there, the light comes back. It also resets emissions readiness, so drive for a few days before a smog test.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do you collect my data?

Your scans stay on your phone. With Help Improve Car Support on, the app sends anonymous facts about which sensors and modules your car model answers on, never your VIN, name or location (Settings ▸ Car Support). With Share Usage Data on, it also sends anonymous usage statistics: device and iOS version, language and region, sessions and the last screen, feature counts (for example "a scan found 2 codes"), paywall and purchase events, and crash reports. Never your name, email, location, VIN, trouble codes, IDFA, prices or anything you type, and your IP address isn't stored. Turn it off in Settings ▸ Privacy.
