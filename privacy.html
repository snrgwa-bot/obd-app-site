Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: your scans, history and car details stay on your iPhone. If you leave Help Improve Car Support on, the app sends anonymous facts about which sensors and modules your car model answers on, never your VIN, name, email or location. With Share Usage Data on, the app also sends anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features are used, crashes) to our own server. Both switches are in Settings and can be turned off any time. There are no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking.

1. Who we are

OBD App is made by Essential Dev Team (Dumitru Bumbu), the data controller for this policy. Contact: essentialdevteam@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you see in the app is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's private storage: scan results and history, trouble codes, freeze-frame data, battery tests, cars in My Cars, maintenance items and the service log, trips, recordings, performance runs, notes and settings. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it. Reports and recordings you share (PDF, CSV, text) go only where you send them.

OBD App uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your OBD-II adapter. It does not use your location.

3. Help Improve Car Support (anonymous car data)

This setting is on by default. After a scan, the app sends:

  • the car's make, model, model year and engine description (decoded from the VIN on your device, see section 5);
  • the OBD protocol the car uses and the vehicle profile the app matched;
  • which car-specific sensors answered and which didn't;
  • which diagnostic modules answered in a Full Scan, with the names they report (for example "Airbag (SRS)");
  • the trouble codes found;
  • the app version and a random installation ID created by the app.
Never sent: your VIN, name, email, phone number, location, Apple ID, the adapter's identity, photos or anything you type, or your live driving data. We don't store or use IP addresses. The installation ID is random and isn't linked to you or your car.

Why: to learn which sensors and modules exist on each model and year, so scans get faster and more accurate for everyone. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app, with a simple opt-out. Where: stored by our backend provider, Supabase (privacy policy). How long: 24 months, then deleted.

Sharing with the OBDb community: OBD App uses the open OBDb vehicle database (obdb.community). We contribute back aggregated findings only, such as "this sensor answered on 12 cars of this model year". These summaries are published openly under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence and never include installation IDs or any per-car record.

Turn it off: Settings ▸ Car Support ▸ Help Improve Car Support. Nothing is collected or sent while it's off. Delete your data: email us the installation ID shown in Settings ▸ Privacy and we'll delete every report from it.

4. Anonymous usage statistics

To see which features help people and to find crashes, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (first-party, no third-party analytics service). Share Usage Data is on by default and you can turn it off any time. What is sent:

  • device model, iOS version and app version;
  • language, region and time zone;
  • accessibility settings in use (for example larger text or VoiceOver), so we can make the app work better with them;
  • sessions (when the app is opened and closed) and the last screen you were on;
  • the app's own events, as counts and categories only: a scan finished (how many codes, how serious, check-engine light on or off), an adapter connected (Bluetooth or the demo car), Live opened, a battery test result (good, weak or bad), a Full Scan finished (how many modules and codes), a code explanation opened (its severity), codes cleared, a recording or trip saved, a gauge alarm went off (which gauge), the gauge theme changed and a report shared;
  • paywall views and which plan was tapped;
  • purchase, trial, renewal-status, refund and restore events (which plan and when, never the price);
  • review-prompt requests;
  • crash and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit.
Never sent: your name, email, phone number, address, location, VIN, trouble codes, car make or model, the adapter's name, the advertising identifier (IDFA) or identifier for vendors (IDFV), prices, receipts or transaction IDs, or anything you type or create. Your IP address is not stored or used. The demo (simulated) car sends no usage events except that it was connected.

Installation ID: a random ID created by the app, used only to group statistics from one install. It isn't linked to you, your Apple ID or your car, and it's deleted with the app. The same ID is used for Help Improve Car Support.

Why: to improve the app and fix crashes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest, with a simple opt-out. Where: our backend provider, Supabase (privacy policy). How long: 13 months (crash and performance reports 6 months), then deleted.

Turn it off: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Share Usage Data. Collection stops at once and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device. Delete your data: email us the installation ID shown in Settings ▸ Privacy and we'll delete everything recorded under it.

6. Purchases and refund requests

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. The app checks your Pro status on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

Refund requests. When someone asks Apple for a refund, Apple may ask us about the purchase. By subscribing, you agree that we may then tell Apple that the purchase was delivered, that a free version or trial was available beforehand, and that we prefer a refund of the unused part. We share nothing else, and only for purchases made after this consent appeared on the subscription screen. We are also notified when a refund is requested or granted, without learning who you are.

7. Cookies, tracking and advertising

OBD App has no ads, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. Its only statistics are the first-party, anonymous usage statistics in section 4, which you can turn off. This website uses no cookies or analytics.

8. Automated decisions

The app explains trouble codes and rates their severity using its built-in database. These are general guidance, not decisions about you, and they're never sent to us (usage statistics only record how serious a code was, never the code).

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Your rights

EU, UK and similar laws (GDPR): you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to processing, restrict it, or receive a copy, and you can complain to your data protection authority. Because the data we hold is anonymous, we can only find it with your installation ID.

California (CCPA/CPRA): we don't sell or share personal information, and you can ask what we hold or ask us to delete it.

11. Children

OBD App isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

12. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Our backend accepts reports and statistics only from the app, limits how many it accepts, and keeps them in storage that no app or public account can read. If a breach ever affected data we hold, we would notify the relevant authorities and publish a notice here as the law requires.

13. Changes

We'll update this page when the app changes how it handles data, and change the date at the top.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: essentialdevteam@gmail.com.
